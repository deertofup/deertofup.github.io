txt

a plain text editor for android

Notes are ordinary .txt files. No account, no sign-up, no proprietary format, and nothing to sign in to. The editor works offline.

what it does

  • opens and edits plain text, including very large files
  • saves as you type, with undo and redo
  • line numbers, find, bookmarks and highlights
  • reads older regional encodings and converts them to UTF-8
  • optional sync to a folder in your own Google Drive
  • fifteen languages

privacy

Notes stay on the device unless you turn sync on, in which case they go to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. There is no server behind this app. Read the privacy policy.
